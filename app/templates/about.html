About this tool

How it works

You answer questions about your data, such as its kind, who funds it and whether it can be open; each answer leads to the next question, and you can go back or skip to the results at any time. The results list the repositories that fit your answers so far: a repository is left out when it contradicts an answer, the others are ordered by the number of answers they support and by how much chemistry they cover, and for what is not known about a repository nothing is assumed. Open a repository to see what is known for each question.
